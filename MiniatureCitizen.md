## Executive Summary

**MiniatureCitizen** is a multi-year museum fundraiser built around one of the most ambitious model-railroad art projects ever attempted: replacing all 1,500 generic HO-scale figures on a 5,000 sq ft layout with custom 3D-printed, hand-painted miniatures of *real people*—each scanned, modeled, printed, documented, painted, shipped, and permanently placed on the railroad in a pose that mirrors an existing scene.

Donors, community members, celebrities, and museum supporters commission a figure of themselves (or a loved one) for a contribution tier. In return they receive a certificate of placement, a numbered limited-edition print of their scan, and a permanent identity on the layout—backed by a searchable registry that maps every figure to its exact location.

The project unfolds over **five calendar years** across eight sequential phases, each governed by documented workflows, quality gates, and a chain-of-custody tracking system robust enough to manage 1,500 individual assets.

## Phase 2 — Recruitment & Human Scanning

**Duration:** Months 3–36 (rolling, concurrent with downstream phases)  
**Lead:** Scanning Coordinator + Community Outreach Manager  

### 2.1 Donor Recruitment
- [ ] Launch public campaign with press release, social assets, and museum event
- [ ] Open online donation portal with tier selection and scheduling system
- [ ] Establish corporate sponsorship track (employee group scans, naming rights)
- [ ] Maintain waitlist for oversubscribed tiers; manage tier rebalancing quarterly
- [ ] Coordinate accessibility outreach: mobility-limited donors (mobile scanning unit)

### 2.2 Pose Matching Process
Before each scan appointment:
1. Retrieve the target Figure ID from the registry
2. Print a reference card showing the existing figure's pose from three angles
3. Confirm the pose is achievable and safe for the donor
4. If donor cannot hold the pose, select nearest suitable open figure slot

### 2.3 Scanning Appointment Workflow

**Appointment duration:** 45–60 minutes per person

| Step | Action | Time |
|------|--------|------|
| Check-in | Verify ID, review and sign image release | 5 min |
| Wardrobe | Change into appropriate clothing (costume rack provided); discuss era/scene | 10 min |
| Calibration | Scanner warm-up, calibration, studio lighting check | 5 min |
| Pose reference | Technician demonstrates target pose; donor rehearses | 5 min |
| Capture | 3–5 scan passes (structured-light or photogrammetry rig) | 10–15 min |
| Review | Preliminary mesh shown to donor for approval; notes recorded | 5 min |
| Documentation | Photo portraits (3 views) for matching verification | 5 min |
| Wrap | Thank-you packet, timeline expectations, portal login | 5 min |

### 2.4 Scanning Technology Notes
- **Preferred:** Structured-light full-body scanner (e.g., Artec Leo, Revopoint RANGE 3, or photogrammetry rig with 50+ cameras)
- **Minimum capture resolution:** 0.5 mm surface detail (detail will be selectively enhanced in modeling)
- **File output:** Raw scan saved as `.ply` or `.obj` with texture; archived immediately to NAS with offsite backup
- **Raw file naming convention:** `MC-{FigureID}_{DonorLastName}_{YYYYMMDD}_RAW.{ext}`

### 2.5 Minor & Accessibility Policies
- Minors (under 18) require parental/guardian consent form and must be accompanied throughout
- Seated, wheelchair-accessible, and reclining scan rigs available upon request
- Mobile scanning unit available for homebound or care-facility donors (Founding tier and Gold+ upon request)

## Phase 3 — Data Processing & 3D Modeling

**Duration:** Months 4–42 (rolling)  
**Lead:** Lead 3D Artist + Digital Pipeline Supervisor  
**Throughput target:** 12–18 figures/week at full staffing  

### 3.1 Mesh Cleanup (per figure — ~3–5 hours)
1. Import raw scan; inspect for holes, noise, and artifacts
2. Decimate mesh to target polygon budget (150k–300k tris for working mesh)
3. Fill holes, repair non-manifold geometry
4. Remove scanner artifacts, noise, floating geometry
5. Verify pose accuracy against reference photo card
6. Save as `MC-{FigureID}_CLEANED.obj`

### 3.2 Scale & Proportion Adjustment
1. Scale model to HO ratio (1:87) and verify against known height of donor
2. Check against target figure slot dimensions on layout (clearance zones)
3. Adjust proportions for print fidelity at scale (facial features, hands, footwear)
4. Save as `MC-{FigureID}_SCALED.obj`

### 3.3 Detail Enhancement & Stylization
HO-scale printing loses fine detail below ~0.3 mm. Compensate by:
- Exaggerating facial feature depth by 15–25% (nose bridge, eye socket, lips)
- Thickening fine elements (fingers, jewelry, straps) to minimum 0.4 mm wall
- Simplifying hair to sculptural planes; texture detail applied via surface normals
- Adding costume period-accuracy if required by scene (conductor uniform, period dress)

### 3.4 Base Design
- Each figure receives a custom integrated base matching the surface type of its location slot (asphalt texture, gravel, grass, platform wood grain, etc.)
- Base includes a micro-engraved Figure ID on the underside (laser etching post-print or embossed in model)
- Magnet mount option for Gold tier and above (rare-earth magnet cavity in base)

### 3.5 Quality Review Gate (Artist → Senior Artist)
- [ ] Likeness accuracy score vs. reference photos (internal rubric, 1–5)
- [ ] Print-readiness check (Meshmixer analysis: overhangs, wall thickness, watertight)
- [ ] Pose accuracy vs. original figure reference card
- [ ] Scale verification at 1:87
- [ ] Approve and promote file to `MC-{FigureID}_PRINTREADY.stl`
- [ ] Reject → flag for rework with notes; max 2 rework cycles before escalation

### 3.6 Donor Preview (Optional — Gold tier and above)
- Generate photorealistic render (Blender Cycles or KeyShot)
- Send to donor via portal with 7-day feedback window
- One round of minor revisions included; structural changes not permitted at this stage

## Phase 4 — 3D Printing & Support Structures

**Duration:** Months 5–44 (rolling)  
**Lead:** Print Farm Manager  
**Throughput target:** 20–30 figures/day at full capacity  

### 4.1 Printer Configuration
- **Recommended printers:** Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra, Phrozen Sonic Mega 8K, or equivalent MSLA
- **Resin type:** ABS-like or Rigid resin (high detail, paintable, impact-resistant)
- **Layer height:** 0.025–0.035 mm (25–35 micron)
- **XY resolution:** 43–50 micron
- **Print bed temperature:** Maintain 25–28°C for consistent curing

### 4.2 Support Structure Generation
- Use automated supports as baseline; hand-edit critical supports on face, hands, and fine details
- Orient figure to minimize support contact on visible surfaces (face, front torso)
- Preferred orientation: slight back-tilt (10–15°) to shed supports from face downward
- Save support file as `MC-{FigureID}_SUPPORTED.lys` (Lychee) or `.chitubox`

### 4.3 Print Batch Management
- Print in batches of 10–25 figures per plate (maximize plate density while maintaining quality)
- Log each print plate: `PLATE-{YYYYMMDD}-{PlateNum}` → list of Figure IDs
- Print log records: start time, end time, resin lot number, ambient temp/RH, layer count
- Failed print protocol: photograph, log failure mode, requeue within 24 hours

### 4.4 Post-Processing
| Step | Method | Time |
|------|--------|------|
| IPA wash | 2-stage wash (Elegoo Mercury Plus or equivalent) — 3 min + 1 min | 4 min |
| Isopropyl drain | Air dry 5 min, check for pooling in cavities | 5 min |
| Support removal | Fine-tip flush cutters, dental picks; under magnification for faces | 15–30 min |
| UV cure | 2 min @ 405 nm, rotate 180°, 2 min more | 5 min |
| Inspection | Magnification (5–10×): check for print lines, support scarring, warping | 10 min |
| Minor repair | Thin CA glue + accelerator for micro-cracks; UV resin pen for pinholes | 0–15 min |

### 4.5 Print Quality Gates
- [ ] No major layer lines visible at 10× magnification in focal areas
- [ ] All supports removed cleanly; no stubs over 0.2 mm
- [ ] No warping: base sits flat within 0.1 mm
- [ ] Pose matches reference card (visual check)
- [ ] Figure ID legible on base underside
- Accept → tag and advance | Reject → photograph defect, reprint, log

## Phase 6 — Painting & Finishing

**Duration:** Months 7–48 (rolling)  
**Lead:** Chief Painter / Painting Program Manager  
**Throughput target:** 40–60 figures/week at full capacity  

### 6.1 Painting Approaches

Three tracks based on available resources and figure tier:

| Track | Method | Who | Tier Eligibility |
|-------|--------|-----|-----------------|
| **In-house** | Museum painting studio; trained volunteers + staff | All tiers |
| **Guild** | Contracted miniature painters (vetted IPMS/NMM artists) | Gold, Platinum, Founding |
| **Donor-commissioned** | Donor selects and pays a listed artist directly | Platinum, Founding (add-on) |

### 6.2 Painting Standard by Tier

| Tier | Standard | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| Bronze | 3-layer block paint + wash + highlight; base texture | Efficient; strong at distance |
| Silver | 4-layer; wet-blend fleshtones; base texture + flock | Improved likeness accuracy |
| Gold | NMM-adjacent; zenithal prime; OSL where appropriate; custom base | Detailed likeness; scene-matched |
| Platinum / Founding | Master-level detail; 10+ layers; hair highlight sculpt; full diorama base | Gallery quality |

### 6.3 Painting Workflow (per figure)
1. **Prime:** Airbrush zenithal prime (dark grey base → grey midtone → white top)
2. **Fleshtone base:** Custom mix matched to donor's reference photo skin tone
3. **Clothing & costume:** Layer by layer, lightest to darkest, then washes
4. **Hair:** Dark base → progressive highlight → gloss or matte finish
5. **Details:** Eyes (key at 1:87; fine liner, 2–3 layers), accessories, insignia
6. **Base:** Texture medium + paint + flock or static grass to match scene
7. **Varnish:** Matte varnish overall; satin on leather/metal; gloss on eyes, wet surfaces
8. **Final QC:** Under 10× magnification; photograph front/back/both sides

### 6.4 Reference Materials per Figure
Each painter receives a packet:
- Front, side, and 3/4 reference photos of donor (from scan appointment)
- Scene photo showing surrounding area and color palette
- Tier standard card
- Clothing/costume reference if historical or uniformed
- Skin tone swatch card matched by scanning tech at appointment

### 6.5 Painting Quality Gates
- [ ] Likeness recognizable at arm's length (informal test: 5 of 5 staff identify donor from portrait)
- [ ] No visible primer through paint in focal areas
- [ ] Eyes correctly placed and symmetrical
- [ ] Base matches adjacent layout surface
- [ ] Varnish uniform; no pooling
- Accept → photograph, log, advance | Return to painter → one revision cycle

## Phase 8 — Removal, Installation & Unveiling

**Duration:** Months 12–60 (rolling; major ceremonies quarterly)  
**Lead:** Layout Curator + Installation Team Lead  

### 8.1 Existing Figure Removal
- Work section by section; never remove more figures than can be replaced within one session
- Photograph each original figure in situ before removal
- Remove with dental pick + isopropyl alcohol (dissolve adhesive)
- Archive original figures by Figure ID in acid-free boxes (historical record)
- Log removal date in Master Registry

### 8.2 Surface Preparation
- Clean adhesive residue from base spot (IPA + cotton swab)
- Repair any damaged scenery disturbed during removal
- Blend in any repainted or re-textured surface to match surroundings

### 8.3 Installation Procedure
1. Retrieve figure by Figure ID from staging tray; verify against registry
2. Dry-fit figure in slot; confirm clearance with adjacent elements
3. Mix two-part epoxy or apply PVA depending on surface type
4. Place figure; hold or brace for cure time
5. Touch up base edge with matching paint + flock
6. Photograph installed figure in situ (wide + macro)
7. Log install date, installer initials, and photo file path in Master Registry
8. Print and laminate scene card (updated figure count for that scene)

### 8.4 Magnet-Mount Installation (Gold+ Optional)
- Drill 2 mm pilot hole in layout surface at figure slot
- Epoxy steel disc flush with surface
- Figure snaps magnetically; removable for cleaning without adhesive damage
- Document in registry: `mount_type = magnet`

### 8.5 Unveiling Ceremonies

Ceremonies are the public-facing heartbeat of the project — they drive media coverage, membership, and new donations.

**Ceremony Cadence:**
| Frequency | Type | Scale |
|-----------|------|-------|
| Quarterly | General Unveiling | 20–50 figures; public event, all tiers |
| Annually | Gala Unveiling | 100–200 figures; ticketed dinner, board + major donors |
| Ad hoc | Special Unveiling | Celebrity figures, milestone counts (figure 500, 1000, 1500) |

**Ceremony Elements:**
- [ ] Invitations mailed 6 weeks out; RSVP by 2 weeks
- [ ] Draped reveal cloth over new figure sections (lifted by donor or family)
- [ ] Personalized story card read aloud for Platinum/Founding figures
- [ ] Museum director remarks; project progress update
- [ ] Guided layout tour; attendees locate their figures
- [ ] Photography station: "Me and My Miniature" portrait with magnifying glass prop
- [ ] Champagne/sparkling cider reception; museum membership table staffed
- [ ] Press release with photos distributed same day
- [ ] Museum blog post + social media content within 48 hours

**The 1,500th Figure Finale:**
The final unveiling is a major event — gala format, media invitations, projected live-stream, remarks from community leaders, and permanent dedication of the layout to the MiniatureCitizen community. Budget and plan 18 months in advance.

### 9.1 Layout Grid & Zone System

#### Zone Definitions

The 5,000 sq ft layout is divided into **10 named geographic Zones**, each reflecting a thematic area of the railroad's world. Zones are the top-level address unit — large enough to be memorable, small enough to be useful.

| Zone Code | Zone Name | Approx. Area | Character |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| `DEPOT` | Union Station | 500 sq ft | Main passenger terminal, platforms, taxi stand |
| `DTOWN` | Downtown | 600 sq ft | Urban streets, shops, office buildings, café tables |
| `YARDS` | Freight Yards | 550 sq ft | Classification yard, freight house, loading docks |
| `INDUS` | Industrial District | 450 sq ft | Factories, warehouses, coal yard |
| `RESID` | Residential | 500 sq ft | Neighborhoods, backyards, corner stores |
| `PARK` | City Park | 350 sq ft | Bandstand, playground, pond, benches |
| `FARM` | Farm Country | 600 sq ft | Cropland, barn, grain elevator, farm road |
| `MOUNT` | Mountain Pass | 500 sq ft | Trestle, tunnel portal, logging camp, ski lodge |
| `LAKE` | Lakeside | 400 sq ft | Marina, resort hotel, fishing pier, beach |
| `JUNCT` | Junction Town | 550 sq ft | Branch-line stop, feed store, water tower |

#### Sector Grid

Each Zone is sub-divided into **Sectors** lettered A–F (not all zones use all letters; assignment follows logical sub-areas like "north side of tracks," "platform level," "street level").

Each Sector contains a numbered grid of **Positions** (01–99). A Position corresponds to a ~2×2 ft area of layout surface — roughly the footprint of a small scene cluster holding 3–8 figures.

**Full figure address format:**
```
{ZONE}-{SECTOR}-{POSITION}
Examples:
  DEPOT-B-07    ← Union Station, Sector B (platform), position 7
  DTOWN-C-22    ← Downtown, Sector C (north block), position 22
  MOUNT-A-03    ← Mountain Pass, Sector A (trestle approach), position 3
```

### 9.3 In-Museum Physical Wayfinding

The digital tool guides visitors to a Viewing Position number. The physical wayfinding system makes that number immediately findable on the museum floor.

#### Viewing Positions (VPs)

**40 Viewing Positions** are defined around the layout perimeter and at peninsula ends, spaced approximately every 7–8 linear feet of accessible layout edge (adjusted for doorways, benches, and accessibility clearances).

Each VP is:
- A numbered floor decal (high-grip vinyl, 12-inch diameter) at the ideal standing position
- A corresponding numbered marker on the layout fascia at eye level (engraved acrylic plate, backlit)
- Visible in the "Find Your Figure" map and app

**VP numbering:** Clockwise from the main entrance, VP-01 through VP-40.

#### Fascia Zone & Sector Signage

Each Zone transition is marked with a **Zone Entry Sign** on the layout fascia:
- Material: Brushed aluminum plate, etched with zone name and icon
- Size: 4 inches × 12 inches
- Mounted at fascia top edge, visible above the layout from standing height

Each Sector is marked with a **Sector Strip** — a color-coded adhesive strip along the fascia base, color corresponding to the zone (consistent palette: DEPOT = navy, DTOWN = burgundy, FARM = green, etc.). Sector letter printed every 12 inches along the strip.

Grid position markers are **not** visibly labeled on the layout itself (too cluttered) — they exist only in the database and digital map. The VP number is the visitor's physical guide.

#### Printed "Figure Finder" Map

A fold-out printed map distributed free at the museum entrance, updated quarterly as new figures are installed.

**Map contents:**
- Front: Overhead schematic of layout with Zone boundaries, VP numbers, and Zone color key
- Back: Alphabetical name index → Grid Address → VP Number (e.g., *"Smith, Jane — DTOWN-C-22 — VP-19"*)
- Scale bar and compass rose
- "How to find your figure" 3-step illustrated guide

**Sizes:** Full A3 (fold to A5 for pocket); also A4 single-sheet version for easy printing at home from the website.

### Appendix J — Viewing Position Layout Planning Guide

When placing VPs during Phase 1 physical setup:

```
Spacing rules:
  • Target 7–8 ft between VP centers along straight layout edges
  • At corners: VP placed at the corner (widest viewing angle)
  • At peninsulas: VP at tip + both sides near base
  • Minimum 36" clear floor depth at each VP (ADA passage clearance)
  • No VP within 24" of a doorway swing arc

Fascia marker height:
  • Standard: 54" from floor to marker center (comfortable standing eye level)
  • Alternate (if fascia is low): mount at top edge of fascia

Floor decal spec:
  • 12" diameter circle, non-slip textured vinyl
  • Zone color background, VP number centered in white, 72-pt bold
  • Applied over clean, dry concrete or tile; anti-slip rated R10

Sight-line check (do before finalizing VP placement):
  • Stand at proposed VP location
  • Confirm figures in assigned grid positions are visible without leaning
  • Note any obstructions (trees, structures, locomotive, overhead catenary)
  • Adjust VP position up to 18" if needed; document final position with tape measure from fixed reference

VP-to-grid coverage map:
  Each VP should cover no more than 60 grid positions (1 grid position ≈ 2×2 ft)
  and no fewer than 10. Adjust sector boundaries if coverage is lopsided.
```
